About Valtron

Valtron Forge & Fittings

Manufacturers and exporters of stainless steel flanges and pipe fittings, based in Mumbai.

Our company

Building on a stainless steel legacy

Valtron Forge & Fittings was founded by Jitendra Kumar as a sister concern of Rishabh Forge Fittings & Mfg. Co., a Mumbai business specialising in stainless steel flanges and fittings.

Rishabh Forge Fittings & Mfg. Co. brings a legacy spanning over 35 years, as described in our company catalogue. Building on this foundation, Valtron continues the focus on quality, reliability and stainless steel solutions for industrial requirements.

Our expertise is in stainless steel flanges and fittings that combine strength, durability and precision. Our understanding of customer requirements supports customised solutions for different applications, with flanges at the centre of our range and buttweld and forged fittings for supporting pipework.

Quality requirements

Define the evidence alongside the product.

A flange requirement includes more than dimensions. The material specification, inspection scope and records requested by the project are part of defining a complete order.

Documentation and testing requirements should be agreed before supply. Certificates, third-party inspection and sector-specific approvals are not assumed to be included unless confirmed for the order.

Review flange standards
Material identity & traceability
State the grade, product specification and certificate requirements. Identify any heat-number traceability or marking expectations in the purchase specification.
Dimensions & joint interfaces
Confirm the applicable standard and edition, flange type, bore, bolt pattern, facing and required finish. Drawing-based details need an approved revision.
Inspection & test scope
List the required checks, acceptance criteria and witness or hold points. Special testing and third-party involvement must be discussed before confirmation.
Protection & delivery records
Include packing, face protection, identification and dispatch-document requirements so the delivery can be checked against the agreed order.

Working with us

From your specification to a defined enquiry.

Whether you are planning an installation or replacing an existing component, a few clear details make the first conversation more useful.

  1. Share the requirement

    Send the flange type, size, material, rating, standard and quantity. For a replacement, include the mating details rather than relying only on a photograph.

  2. Resolve the details

    Provide drawings and revisions where needed. Confirm inspection records, special conditions and any customer approvals that affect the scope.

  3. Agree the supply scope

    Discuss availability, delivery location and schedule with the team. Technical requirements and commercial terms are confirmed for the individual order.
