Threaded Cross Manufacturer, Supplier & Exporter

Valtron Forge & Fittings is a leading manufacturer, supplier, stockist, and exporter of premium quality Threaded Cross in India. We offer a comprehensive range designed to meet international standards and the demanding requirements of industrial piping systems.

Our threaded crosses connect four pipes at a single point, distributing flow in three directions from one inlet – a compact solution for manifold and header assemblies.

A Threaded Cross is a forged four-way fitting with all outlets on a common plane at 90 degrees to each other. Manufactured to ASME B16.11, it is used in fire-fighting networks, instrument manifolds and distribution headers where multiple take-offs meet at one point.

Key Features

Four-way junction with outlets at 90° intervals

Compact alternative to multiple tee assemblies

Pressure classes 2000#, 3000# and 6000# as per ASME B16.11

Forged one-piece construction with reinforced junction

Leak-tight NPT / BSPT / BSPP thread options

Available in all grades of stainless, carbon, alloy and nickel alloys

Sizes from 1/8” NB to 4” NB

Material Used in Manufacturing

  • We manufacture Threaded Fittings in a wide range of materials depending on service conditions and project specifications:
  • Stainless Steel (ASTM A182 F304/304L, F316/316L, F321, F347)
  • Carbon Steel (ASTM A105)
  • Alloy Steel (ASTM A182 F1, F5, F9, F11, F22, F91)
  • Low Temperature Carbon Steel (ASTM A350 LF2 / LF3)
  • Duplex & Super Duplex Stainless Steel (ASTM A182 F51 / F53 / F55)
  • Nickel Based Alloys (ASTM B564 – Monel, Inconel, Incoloy, Hastelloy)
  • All materials comply with standards issued by ASTM International and ASME.

Applications

  • Threaded fittings are ideal for:
  • Low and medium pressure piping systems
  • Instrumentation, sampling and gauge connections
  • Utility lines – air, water, steam and gas services
  • Fire protection and sprinkler systems
  • Oil & gas surface piping where welding is not desirable
  • Plumbing and general industrial piping networks
