Lap Joint Flanges Manufacturer, Supplier & Exporter

Valtron Forge & Fittings is a renowned manufacturer and exporter of Lap Joint Flanges, based in Mumbai, India. Our comprehensive range of Lap Joint Flanges is precisely engineered to meet diverse piping requirements across various industries.

At Valtron Forge & Fittings, we manufacture Lap Joint Flanges in a wide selection of materials, including Stainless Steel, Carbon Steel, Alloy Steel, Duplex Steel, Super Duplex Steel, and Nickel-based alloys, ensuring suitability for demanding applications.

Our Lap Joint Flanges are produced in compliance with international standards such as ANSI, JIS, DIN, EN 1092-1, UNI, BS 4504, SABS 1123, AS 2129, and GOST 12820, and are available in a wide range of sizes. All products are manufactured under strict International Quality Standards (IQS) to ensure superior performance, durability, and reliability.

Additionally, we offer custom-designed Lap Joint Flanges tailored to meet our clients’ specific technical requirements.

A Stub end Flange or more commonly called Lap Joint Flange is used with Stub Ends. A Stub End adapter slides through the Flange and is welded to the pipe. The Lap Joint Flange is similar to the Slip On Flange except it is flat across the face and has a radius machined at the bore. This allows it to rotate freely around the Stub for easy alignment to the mating Flange.

Lap Joint Flanges are supplied in all Diameters and pressure classes by Valtron Forge & Fittings

It is available in almost all pipe diameters.

Ideal for systems where frequent disassembly of the flange is required e.g. when cleaning or inspection is required.

It is possible to use a cheaper grade material for the lap joint flange e.g. carbon steel because only the stub end must match the associated pipe material/grade; this can result in considerable cost savings.
