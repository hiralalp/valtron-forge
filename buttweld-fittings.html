Buttweld Fittings

Valtron Forge & Fittings is a leading manufacturer, stockist, and exporter of high-quality buttweld fittings engineered to comply with international standards such as ASTM, ASME, DIN, and EN. We provide a comprehensive range of buttweld fittings including Elbows, Tees, Reducers, Caps, and Crosses, available in Stainless Steel, Carbon Steel, Alloy Steel, and other high-performance materials. Our buttweld fittings are precision-engineered for excellent strength, smooth flow characteristics, and long-term reliability in demanding environments. Widely used in oil & gas, petrochemical, power plants, marine, and water treatment sectors, our products are renowned for their perfect dimensional accuracy, corrosion resistance, and leak-proof welding performance in high-pressure piping systems.
