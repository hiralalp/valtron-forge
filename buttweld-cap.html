Buttweld Pipe Cap Manufacturer, Supplier & Exporter

Valtron Forge & Fittings is a leading manufacturer, supplier, stockist, and exporter of premium quality Buttweld Pipe Cap in India. We offer a comprehensive range designed to meet international standards and the demanding requirements of industrial piping systems.

Our buttweld caps permanently seal the end of a pipeline while withstanding full line pressure, providing a smooth ellipsoidal closure with no crevices or dead legs.

A Buttweld Cap is an ellipsoidal (torispherical) end closure welded directly to the pipe end. Unlike threaded plugs, the welded cap provides a permanent, hermetic, full-pressure seal suitable for hydrotesting, future extensions and end-of-line terminations.

Key Features

Permanent, leak-proof closure for pipe ends

Ellipsoidal profile withstands full line pressure

Smooth internal surface with no crevices or dead legs

Uniform wall thickness matching the connecting pipe schedule

Bevelled ends as per ASME B16.25 for full-penetration welding

Available in all grades of stainless, carbon, alloy and nickel alloys

Suitable for high-pressure and high-temperature applications

  • Stainless Steel (ASTM A403 WP304/304L, WP316/316L, WP321, WP347)
  • Carbon Steel (ASTM A234 WPB / WPC)
  • Alloy Steel (ASTM A234 WP1, WP5, WP9, WP11, WP22, WP91)
  • Low Temperature Carbon Steel (ASTM A420 WPL3 / WPL6)
  • Duplex & Super Duplex Stainless Steel (ASTM A815 UNS S31803 / S32205 / S32750)
  • Nickel Based Alloys (ASTM B366 – Monel, Inconel, Incoloy, Hastelloy)
  • All materials comply with standards issued by ASTM International and ASME.
