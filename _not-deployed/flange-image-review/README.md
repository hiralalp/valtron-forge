# Flange and Fitting Image Review

Updated 12 September 2026. **92 original images downloaded: five from Wikimedia Commons and 87 supplier references.** This total excludes generated contact sheets and existing website assets. No download has been published or finally approved for Valtron's catalogue.

These are review assets, not a complete catalogue image library. No website page references these downloads. Keep this directory out of deployment; its name alone does not enforce that exclusion.

The two blocked pages are **Lip-Type Flanges** and **High Hub Blind Flanges**. Lip-type thumbnails appear to show seals; the inspection photograph does not establish the design. High-hub supplier images show blind faces without a profile proving a high hub. Obtain an accurate client photo, supplier-authorised photograph, or approved drawing for each. Do not fill these positions with generic flanges.

Fifty pages having a reference does **not** mean fifty pages have publication-ready product images. The 6 facing, 16 material, 18 standard/range, and 8 hub/guide pages in the wider plan are not included in this 52-row product register. Their image assignments remain pending: use relevant approved flange photos and accurate facing/assembly details, with shared imagery where appropriate. A photograph cannot establish an alloy grade, pressure rating, or standard compliance.

## Supplier References

[supplier-references/sources.json](supplier-references/sources.json) records source pages, direct image URLs, original supplier captions, download dates, dimensions, SHA-256 checksums and permission status for all 87 supplier images. These are **permission-pending**, not stock photography licensed for Valtron's website. Do not remove watermarks, supplier names, embedded certifications or marks to make them appear to be Valtron products.

Sources researched include Dynamic Forge & Fittings, Marcel Piping, Metallica / Steel Pipes Factory, Fit-Lok, TP-Products and Micron Steel. Candidate URLs are in [supplier-candidates.json](supplier-candidates.json); downloads are reproducible with [supplier-download.ps1](supplier-download.ps1). A source caption is preserved as evidence, not endorsed as accurate technical information.

The enlarged batch includes anchor, swivel ring, compact, integral compact-system, SAE split, separate Weldo/Nipo, separate spade/spacer, and custom-drawing references. It also adds buttweld angle/radius drawings, return bends, reducers, tees, stub ends, caps, and dedicated forged elbow, cap, plug and reducing-insert candidates.

Visual review identified incorrect existing assignments to replace during website implementation:

- The short-radius 45-degree buttweld page uses a compression elbow photograph.
- The long-radius 45-degree buttweld page uses a forged fitting reference, not buttweld.
- The forged-elbow photograph shows tees.
- The reducing-insert photograph shows threaded bushings, not socket-weld reducing inserts.
- The existing orifice photo does not show pressure taps; its engineering drawing is the stronger reference until an assembly photo is approved.

Other exclusions: generic machining scenes are not exact specialty-product photos; ordinary blind faces do not prove high hubs; compact-system integral cutaways do not represent all integral flanges; SAE split clamps do not represent all split flange designs. Small supplier material thumbnails cannot prove material composition. All four lip-type references are explicitly rejected in their source records.

## Publication Gate

Every published product page must have at least one accurate, rights-cleared product image or approved illustration, with meaningful alt text and a relevant detail view where needed. Do not publish an empty placeholder or knowingly mismatched image to satisfy a count.

The remaining work is permission/provenance clearance, client confirmation of specialty designs, replacement of low-resolution and diagram-only primary assets, approved web exports, and page integration. A client-approved CAD render is acceptable when clearly identified as an illustration; it must not be presented as a photograph of manufactured stock.

## Reproducible Verification

Run from the repository root:

```powershell
& .\_not-deployed\flange-image-review\download.ps1 -VerifyOnly
& .\_not-deployed\flange-image-review\supplier-download.ps1 -VerifyOnly
```

These check decoding, recorded dimensions, checksums and required source/license or permission metadata. They are not technical product-certification or legal-clearance checks.

Running [download.ps1](download.ps1) without `-VerifyOnly` downloads missing entries on a fresh setup. On repeat runs it preserves files with matching source records and checksums and refuses to overwrite unverified files. It does not silently repair a missing file already recorded in the manifest.

For supplier WebP validation, install the temporary decoder outside the public website, then run downloads or regenerate the review sheets:

```powershell
npm install --prefix "$env:TEMP\valtron-image-tools" --no-save --no-package-lock sharp@0.34.5
& .\_not-deployed\flange-image-review\supplier-download.ps1 -Products all
& .\_not-deployed\flange-image-review\contact-sheets.ps1
```

[image-info.cjs](image-info.cjs) uses Node and Sharp to decode WebP and create temporary PNG previews for contact sheets. Originals remain unchanged. Failed downloads emit warnings; inspect them rather than interpreting integrity verification of existing files as proof that every candidate was downloaded. Windows PowerShell 5.1 and System.Drawing handle the JPEG/PNG checks. No npm dependencies were added to the public website.
