Barrel Nipple Manufacturer, Supplier & Exporter

Valtron Forge & Fittings is a leading manufacturer, supplier, stockist, and exporter of premium quality Barrel Nipple in India. We offer a comprehensive range designed to meet international standards and the demanding requirements of industrial piping systems.

Our barrel nipples provide a short externally threaded pipe section with a plain central barrel, used to join fittings and extend threaded connections throughout utility piping.

A Barrel Nipple is a short length of pipe threaded on both ends with an unthreaded barrel in the middle, manufactured from seamless pipe or forged bar as per BS 1387 / IS 1239 / ASME B36.10. Supplied in lengths from close pattern up to 600 mm.

Key Features

Externally threaded both ends with plain central barrel

Manufactured from seamless pipe or forged bar stock

Available in standard, close and custom lengths

Heavy, medium and light wall classes

Leak-tight NPT / BSPT / BSPP thread options

Available in all grades of stainless, carbon, alloy and nickel alloys

Sizes from 1/8” NB to 4” NB

Material Used in Manufacturing

  • We manufacture Threaded Fittings in a wide range of materials depending on service conditions and project specifications:
  • Stainless Steel (ASTM A182 F304/304L, F316/316L, F321, F347)
  • Carbon Steel (ASTM A105)
  • Alloy Steel (ASTM A182 F1, F5, F9, F11, F22, F91)
  • Low Temperature Carbon Steel (ASTM A350 LF2 / LF3)
  • Duplex & Super Duplex Stainless Steel (ASTM A182 F51 / F53 / F55)
  • Nickel Based Alloys (ASTM B564 – Monel, Inconel, Incoloy, Hastelloy)
  • All materials comply with standards issued by ASTM International and ASME.

Applications

  • Threaded fittings are ideal for:
  • Low and medium pressure piping systems
  • Instrumentation, sampling and gauge connections
  • Utility lines – air, water, steam and gas services
  • Fire protection and sprinkler systems
  • Oil & gas surface piping where welding is not desirable
  • Plumbing and general industrial piping networks
