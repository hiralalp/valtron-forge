Angle / Beam / Channels

Valtron Forge & Fittings is a trusted manufacturer, stockist, and exporter of premium-quality Angles, Beams, and Channels, engineered to meet international standards such as ASTM, ASME, DIN, and ISO. We offer a comprehensive range of structural sections including Equal Angles, Unequal Angles, I-Beams, H-Beams, U-Channels, and custom profiles, available in Stainless Steel, Carbon Steel, Alloy Steel, and other high-performance materials. Our structural components are known for their excellent load-bearing capacity, precise dimensions, and superior corrosion resistance, making them suitable for construction, infrastructure, fabrication, oil & gas, shipbuilding, and industrial machinery applications. With strict quality control and on-time delivery, we deliver dependable structural solutions tailored to your project requirements.
