Flanges

Explore connection types, compare their construction and specify the details for your project.

23 flange types

Drawing-based enquiry

Welded connections

Weld Neck Flanges

A tapered hub carries the connection from the flange body to a butt-welded pipe end. The pipe bore and wall thickness are important parts of the flange specification.

Welded connections

Slip-On Flanges

The flange bore slips over the pipe before the connection is secured with fillet welds. Pipe outside diameter and installation clearance govern the fit.

Closure & measurement

Blind Flanges

A solid flange closes a flanged opening without a through bore. Its bolted connection allows the closure to be removed for access when the system is safely isolated.

Mechanical connections

Threaded Flanges

Internal threads connect the flange to a matching threaded pipe end. The thread form, nominal size and mating pipe specification must be stated together.

Welded connections

Socket Weld Flanges

A recessed socket receives the pipe end, with a fillet weld at the outside of the hub. Socket depth, bore and assembly requirements distinguish it from a slip-on connection.

Mechanical connections

Lap Joint Flanges

A backing flange works with a separate stub end or lapped pipe end. The stub end provides the gasket seating surface while the backing flange transmits the bolt load.

Welded connections

Long Weld Neck Flanges

An extended neck combines a flanged connection with a longer nozzle section. Neck length, bore and end preparation must be specified for the intended equipment connection.

Closure & measurement

Orifice Flanges

Pressure-tapping connections distinguish an orifice flange from a general pipe flange. A matched assembly supports the differential-pressure measurement arrangement around an orifice plate.

Mechanical connections

Companion Flanges

A companion flange is selected to mate with a particular flanged connection. The term describes its role in an assembly, so the actual connection form and dimensions must be identified.

Construction & special designs

Plate Flanges

A plate flange is a ring-shaped component made from plate, with an open bore and a bolt pattern specified for the mating connection. Plate construction is distinct from a flat gasket facing.

Welded connections

Reducing Flanges

A reducing flange combines a larger mating-flange interface with a smaller pipe connection or bore. Both the mating size and the reduced connection must be identified.

Branch connections

Weldo Flanges

A Weldo flange combines a flanged branch connection with a weld-on outlet form at the run pipe. The branch-to-run interface is a defining part of the requirement.

Branch connections

Nipo Flanges

A Nipo flange is a flanged branch-outlet form with a nipple-like extended neck. Projection and the run-pipe attachment must be defined as part of the complete branch.

Construction & special designs

Forged Flanges

Forged flanges describe a manufacturing route spanning several connection forms, including weld neck, slip-on and blind. The flange type must still be specified.

Mechanical connections

Loose Flanges

A loose flange is a separate backing component working with a collar, lapped end or stub end. It transfers bolt load while the companion component forms the pipe-side seating interface.

Construction & special designs

Square Flanges

A square flange has a square external form and a connection-specific bolt arrangement. The outer shape alone does not identify its bore, seal or pressure rating.

Welded connections

Expander Flanges

An expander flange incorporates a transition between a smaller pipe-side connection and a larger flanged bore. The transition geometry is part of the component design.

Closure & measurement

High Hub Blind Flanges

A high hub blind flange combines a closed centre with a projecting hub on its rear side. The hub profile distinguishes it from an ordinary flat-backed blind flange.

Closure & measurement

Spectacle Blinds

A spectacle blind joins a solid blank and an open ring with a connecting bridge. One side or the other is positioned in the flange joint to define the line condition.

Closure & measurement

Spades / Paddle Blinds

A spade is a solid line blank with a handle, installed between flanges for a specified isolation condition. It is used with a separately identified matching spacer arrangement.

Closure & measurement

Ring Spacers

A ring spacer is the open component of a separate spade-and-spacer arrangement. Its bore permits the intended flow path while maintaining the specified flange-joint spacing.

Construction & special designs

Lip-Type Flanges

Lip-type flange enquiries require a drawing of the intended lip and mating arrangement. The name alone does not establish a unique connection, seal system or standard.

Construction & special designs

Puddle Flanges

A puddle flange is a collar arrangement at a pipe penetration through a wall or slab. It addresses the path around the outside of the pipe rather than a conventional bolted pipe joint.
