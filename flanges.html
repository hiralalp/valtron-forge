Flanges

Valtron Forge & Fittings is a leading manufacturer, stockist, and exporter of high-quality flanges designed to meet global standards such as ASTM, ASME, DIN, and EN. We offer a comprehensive range of flanges including Weld Neck, Slip-On, Blind, Socket Weld, Lap Joint, and Threaded Flanges, available in Stainless Steel, Carbon Steel, Alloy Steel, and other high-performance materials. Our flanges are engineered for strength, precision, and durability, ensuring leak-proof connections in critical piping systems. Widely used in oil & gas, petrochemical, power plants, water treatment, and marine industries, our products are trusted worldwide for their reliability, dimensional accuracy, and corrosion resistance.
