Orifice Flanges are essential components used to measure the flow of fluids in piping systems. When an Orifice Plate is installed, it creates a flow restriction that generates differential pressure, enabling accurate measurement of liquids, steam, or gases. An Orifice Plate is a thin, precision-engineered plate with a central bore that determines the flow rate.

Orifice Flanges come with either raised faces or ring-type joint (RTJ ) facings.

Orifice flanges have radially tapped holes in the flange ring to connect meters.

There are orifice plate carriers in these flanges for the reason of fitting meter connections.

The additional bolts available serve as a jack to allow the separation of the two flanges to change the orifice plate or for other relevant services like an inspection.l

Orifice Flanges are made up of various materials, but they depend on the environment and stress to which they are subjected.
