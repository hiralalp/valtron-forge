## Agreed Direction

- Position Valtron Forge & Fittings as a flange-focused business: approximately 90% of product showcase emphasis on flanges, with the remaining 10% shared between buttweld fittings and forged fittings.
- Maximize useful flange range coverage, with an individual, directly accessible page for every distinct product type and substantial pages for construction, facing, material, and standard ranges.
- Treat all reference-derived flange ranges as planned scope, not optional menu mentions. Add separately identified specialty ranges to broaden the showcase beyond that reference.
- Retain exactly three visible product categories: Flanges, Buttweld Fittings, and Forged Fittings. Remove all other product categories from navigation, homepage promotions, product sidebars, footer product links, search/filter choices, and related-product recommendations.
- Keep buttweld and forged category/detail pages accessible as supporting ranges. Preserve other legacy product URLs without actively promoting them; do not delete their pages or redirect them to unrelated products.
- Prepare and approve the catalogue before implementing website changes.
- Preserve the current brand and visual language unless a separate redesign is approved.

### Product Page Template

Each flange page should contain the product name, accurate photograph, original description, relevant applications, approved materials, applicable standards, validated dimensions/ratings, available facings, and a quote CTA carrying the product name. Flange-page sidebars and related items should prioritize flanges, with at most a compact link to the two supporting fitting ranges. Fitting pages should link to relevant products within their own retained family and offer a clear route back to the flange catalogue. No sidebar or related-product area may promote a removed category.

For each product type, plan a primary product photograph, useful detail views, a labelled dimensional drawing where available, and responsive specification tables. Images should have consistent framing, descriptive alternative text, and appropriate lazy loading below the first viewport. Dimensions must come from approved data, not inferred photograph proportions.

Use type-specific content: material pages explain the material; standards pages explain dimensional scope; facing pages explain mating requirements; product pages explain geometry and application. Each needs a unique title, primary heading, description, canonical URL, and breadcrumb trail. Add only accurate structured data; no invented prices, stock status, reviews, or certificates.

Specialty products without approved photographs must not be illustrated with a different flange presented as that product. Request client photographs or licensed, technically accurate alternatives.
